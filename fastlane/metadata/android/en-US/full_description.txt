Puki is a gentle game world for toddlers (about 2 to 4), in Hebrew and English, with a friendly parrot who cheers them on.

FREE, FOREVER
No price, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no "full version" and no ads. Nothing ever stops the fun to ask for money. Puki is open source (MIT), made by a parent who got tired of toddler games that do.

THE GAMES
• Animal Garden: feed each animal the right food
• Colors, Shapes, Counting and Letters, with spoken prompts
• Music: drum, xylophone, tambourine and maracas
• My Day: bath, meals, getting dressed, tidying up, brushing teeth and bedtime
• Drawing on an easel, and a sticker album that fills up with stars
• Peekaboo barn: find the hiding animal by its sound
• Echo Puki: say anything and Puki says it back in a silly parrot voice
• Say it with Puki: a picture, a word, and the child says it back

MADE FOR TODDLERS
No reading needed, big tap targets, and no failing: a wrong answer gets a gentle shake and "let's try again". Hebrew instructions match the child (girl or boy). When the time limit the parents set is up, there's no countdown: evening falls, Puki yawns, waves goodbye, and the music fades.

PUKI CAN TALK (OPTIONAL)
Turn on Puki's brain in the parent settings and he comes alive: he says something new about what the child just did, listens when the child talks to him, and lets the child answer a game's question out loud ("blue!") instead of tapping. His brain runs on the tablet itself: small models, two of them trained just for him, with no internet and no cloud. It's a one-time download of about half a GB, and it runs on ordinary tablets with 3 GB of memory. For now the brain speaks Hebrew. Without it, Puki uses his recorded voice and every game works.

KEPT ON A SHORT LEASH
Puki's lines are short and written for a toddler. If the child mentions hurt, fear or a big question, Puki gives a fixed, gentle answer that points to mommy or daddy. Every line he writes is checked, and a line that fails is replaced by a recorded one. Parents can see the latest lines Puki said and flag anything that isn't right.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The child's voice, drawings and name never leave the device, and recordings are never kept. Settings sit behind a parent gate.
